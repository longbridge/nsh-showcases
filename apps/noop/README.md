# Noop

Your own AI team, so your day can be a no-op.

Noop runs a small team of Claude Code and Codex agents on your own computers. They do your daily work, decide what they can based on how you've decided before, and only ask you when something really needs you, with a short list of options. The client is built with Native Shell.

## Give it a goal

One sentence is enough. Noop breaks the work down, runs it in parallel and comes back with results. It starts cautious and asks more early on, then takes over more as it proves it decides like you. When it needs you, you get a notification with the choices, and you answer right there.

## Quiet by design

You hear about results and decisions, nothing else. The team channel shows what the agents report, and decision cards lay out the question, the evidence and the options, with a recommendation.

## Your machines, your account

Noop runs on your machines and your own free Cloudflare account, with no shared service and no one else's server. One always-on Linux machine is the primary, and other machines claim work when they're online. Each machine has its own page with its load, memory, disk, signed-in tools and current work, and you can update them all from one place.

## Routines and rules

Recurring work such as triaging notifications, reviewing pull requests and writing reports runs on a schedule you control. Versions of the team's shared files stay in history for up to 90 days and can be reverted, including the rules the team edits for itself.

## On every device

Follow the team from the app on macOS, Linux, Windows, Android and iOS, or open your Noop address in a browser.
